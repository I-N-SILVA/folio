# QLICO Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity

**Name:** QLICO (always uppercase).
**Tagline:** The intelligent document format.
**Headline:** Turn PDFs into interactive experiences.

### Tone & Voice
- **Direct & Confident:** We don’t over-explain. The product speaks for itself.
- **Frictionless:** Every interaction feels effortless and intuitive.
- **Modern & Premium:** High contrast, massive whitespace, and sharp typography.
- **Uncluttered:** We remove anything that isn't absolutely essential.

---

## 2. Visual Identity

### Colors (Stark Minimal)
QLICO's palette is based on extreme contrast and clarity. We reject unnecessary gradients and complex backgrounds in favor of pure whites and deep blacks.

| Token | Light Mode | Dark Mode | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `background` | `#ffffff` | `#000000` | Pure stark background |
| `foreground` | `#000000` | `#ffffff` | Primary text |
| `accent` | `#000000` | `#ffffff` | Primary actions and highlights |
| `qlico-muted` | `#888888` | `#888888` | Secondary text |

### Typography
We use sharp, modern grotesque typography for maximum clarity.

- **Display & Body:** `Inter` (or similar). Extremely clean, highly legible, perfectly geometric. No serif distractions.

---

## 3. The Logo

- The logo mark is a Q built from a document overlapping an eye/lens.
- Standard layout: mark on the left, QLICO logotype on the right.
- Both the mark and logotype are monochrome (black on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds) to match the stark aesthetic.
